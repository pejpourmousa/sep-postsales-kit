Overview: Harborline is a diversified U.S. alcohol company that pioneered modern craft brewing and has since built a “beyond beer” portfolio spanning hard tea, hard seltzer, cider, and RTD spirits-based cocktails. The center of gravity has shifted decisively: Tangle Tea and Clearwave together drive the large majority of dollar sales, with Tangle Tea now the primary growth engine and Clearwave in structural decline alongside the broader hard seltzer category[1,10].
