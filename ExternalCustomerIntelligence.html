What it does

Researches the industry and category of a selected customer to provide external context, helping identify whitespace opportunities and prepare for renewal conversations, QBRs, and standard engagement moments.

Best used for

2. Renewed Group Strategy (Strategic Review & Capital Markets Event)

Confidence: High  |  Last updated: 11 Feb 2026 (event); referenced in Aug 2026 FY26 report

Strategic review ran April 2024 – December 2025. Key outcome: Northfield retained its Africa business (judged offers didn’t reflect underlying value) rather than exiting, citing long-term demographic tailwinds (Africa population +900m projected over 25 years; its largest African market +100m alone). The review also included the sale of its 50% stake in a regional joint venture to its JV partner for $70m.

The renewed Group strategy, set out at the 11 Feb 2026 Capital Markets Event, centers the business on three core categories — Personal, Home, Baby Care — across four lead markets (UK, ANZ, West Africa, Southeast Asia), balanced between developed and emerging economies. Two stated pillars: (1) building winning portfolios of locally-loved brands, and (2) leveraging go-to-market capabilities and manufacturing scale. Target: double-digit total shareholder returns through the cycle.

New capital allocation policy: adjusted net debt/EBITDA target of 1.0–1.5x; bolt-on M&A to be weighed against direct shareholder returns once that range is reached.

FY26 is being presented internally as the first proof point: The CEO specifically cited Solace Glow’s return to growth in North America as early validation. The FY26 Annual Report also flags “strengthened New Product Development (NPD) for this year and next” — a direct opening for a research/testing conversation.

Source: Investor news coverage; company RNS; FY26 Annual Report
Gap flagged: No granular category-by-category investment breakdown or specific NPD pipeline detail was found beyond the above. The Capital Markets Event materials on the company’s own site are the primary source for deeper detail.
