Agent overview

Account 360Beta

A 360° view of any account from BI-MCP — history with us, products used, open pipeline, account plan and more — shaped to the question you’re asking, not a fixed template.

What it does

A 360 view of an account, pulling from BI-MCP to cover history with us, products used, open pipeline, account plan details, and more. Output is structured based on the specific need rather than a fixed format.

Data sources

  • BI-MCP: BU & customer attributes
  • Bookings, revenue & credit remaining
  • Account plans, white space & success plans
  • Cases & contacts (budget holders, power users)
  • Gong call briefs (prior-night sync)

Best used for

  • Account takeovers & handoffs
  • Renewal and expansion planning
  • Spotting single-threading & concentration risk
  • Exec/QBR prep with internal context

Output shape

No fixed template. The agent structures the output around the specific need — e.g. a single-BU brief, a renewal prep view, or (below) a full multi-BU account takeover.

How it works

Describe the neede.g. “full overview of every Meridian BU for an account takeover”.
Resolve the accountMatches HQ and BU records, flagging stubs and mismatched IDs.
Pull from BI-MCPPlans, bookings, revenue, cases, contacts, success plans and Gong briefs.
Shape the outputRollups, per-BU tables and risks — structured to the request.
Rate & show gapsConfidence per section; nulls reported as “No data available in BI-MCP”.
Request behind the example: Account Takeover / Full Overview — all 8 active Meridian Foods business units, rolled up with per-BU detail.

10. Risks — evidenced, by category

Confidence: Medium (synthesized across several sources)  |  Last updated: 2026-09-10

Single-threading. Spain relies on one contact (Lucia Ortega) who is simultaneously the only budget holder and only power user on record. Austria has zero budget holders on record across all 6 contacts. UK and France each carry our own success-plan label of “limited contact.” Source: ai_business_unit_contacts_model, ai_customer_account_success_plans_model.

Solution concentration. US Insights’ revenue is heavily concentrated in one product line — Rapid Insights accounts for roughly 55% of its all-time revenue ($4.08M of ~$7.4M across all lines). Global Insights is similarly concentrated in Platform Access (the platform fee itself, ~54% of its revenue). A renewal risk on either line is a revenue-concentration risk for the whole BU. Source: ai_revenue_model.

Team / stakeholder turnover. The HQ account plan explicitly flags a leadership transition risk: “Paul’s replacement coming in from CA and historically not very engaged with us.” Gong calls independently corroborate a Global Insights leadership change in progress (new director, Elena onboarding) as of mid-2026. Source: ai_customer_account_plans_summary_model; ai_gong_calls_model.

Austria — activity possibly mis-tracked, not absent. Austria shows zero bookings, zero revenue, zero cases, and zero Gong calls under its own account_id, despite having active contacts (2 power users). A Germany-account booking titled “Meridian Austria Pilot” ($21,638, closed April 2026) suggests Austria work is being run and recorded under the Germany account rather than genuinely absent. This should be confirmed with Tom Keller (Germany/Austria CS Lead) rather than read as “no relationship.” Source: ai_bookings_model, ai_business_unit_contacts_model.

Competitor exposure. Checked and not clear: the HQ account plan notes it is “still tough to compete with some regional competitors on pricing,” and Canada’s most recent Gong call (March 2026) references losing a naming project to a named competitor on pricing/customizability grounds. This is an active, not merely historical, competitive pressure point. Source: ai_customer_account_plans_summary_model; ai_gong_calls_model (Canada, 2026-03-13).

Sentiment drift / follow-through. No numeric sentiment field exists in ai_gong_calls_model, so this is a qualitative, Medium-confidence read. Recurring, unresolved complaints about Product Tracker/reporting data access surface repeatedly across more than a dozen Global Insights calls between 2024 and 2025, and the US Insights team’s own account plan admits “cadence calls... fell off... after becoming too repetitive/box-checking.” Combined with the Sep 2026 internal framing of US reporting work as a retention play, this points to real relationship strain in the two largest BUs specifically — not a portfolio-wide pattern.
