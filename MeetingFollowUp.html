Agent overview

Meeting Follow UpBeta

Turns your latest Gong call into a ready-to-send follow-up in your brand’s tone of voice — with the right asset attached — and only sends once you approve.

What it does

Pulls recent Gong transcripts and drafts a follow-up email in the company’s tone of voice. If the transcript identifies a relevant asset, it queries the website and Highspot to recommend one. The draft is pushed to email only after user approval.

Data sources

  • Gong call transcripts (most recent meetings)
  • Company website content
  • Highspot asset library
  • Brand tone-of-voice guidance

Best used for

  • Same-day meeting follow-ups
  • Recapping agreed plans, owners & open asks
  • Sharing the right collateral at the right moment
  • Keeping follow-ups consistent across the team

How it works

Select the meetingAgent pulls the latest Gong transcript for the account.
Extract the substanceDecisions, owners, open asks and topics raised on the call.
Match assetsIf a relevant asset came up, it searches the website and Highspot.
Draft in brand voiceConcise, warm, specific — ready for the rep to review.
Human approvalNothing is sent. The draft is pushed to email only once the user approves.
