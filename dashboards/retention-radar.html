  1. Segment gap: SMB accounts are churning at 11.6% of segment ARR this quarter vs. 1.7% for Enterprise and 6.7% for Mid-Market — the pattern points to an onboarding or time-to-value gap in smaller accounts, not a product-wide issue.
  2. Biggest single leak: budget/price-driven churn ($68K) plus unrealized ROI ($34K) — together 49% of this quarter's hard-churn dollars — concentrated in Mid-Market and SMB renewals.
  3. Product signal: Core Platform carries the largest absolute hard-churn dollars ($95K), but that's expected since it's on every contract; Integrations Hub's churn is small in dollars but high relative to its own ARR base — worth a closer look at that module's adoption.
  4. Early warning: $140K of ARR moved from Yellow to Red health in the last 30 days, and 152 accounts currently sit on a red/yellow adoption-score trigger task with no action logged yet.
  5. Recommended action: clear the "no AM/CSM activity in 30 days" backlog (134 accounts) first — it's the largest lever CSMs directly control, and several of those accounts overlap with the Yellow→Red health movers above.
