Methodology & benchmarks

Definitions

  • Multi-year (MY): a closed-won contract with term_months > 12. Term buckets: 1 Year (≤12), 2 Years (13–24), 3 Years (25–36), 4 Years (37–48), 5+ Years (>48).
  • MY renewal rate: MY closed-won renewals ÷ all closed-won renewals in the close period (by count, or by ARR when Measure = ARR).
  • Protected in year Y: closed-won contracts with close date before Jan 1 of Y and contract end (start_date + term_months) on or after Jan 1 of Y+1. Accounts are counted distinctly; ARR is summed.
  • 1Y → MY conversion: share of closed-won renewals whose prior contract was 12 months that re-signed on a multi-year term.
  • Churn at renewal: closed-lost renewals ÷ all closed renewals (won + lost), split by the prior contract's term.
  • Avg MY term: mean term_months across closed-won MY deals (renewals and new business) in the period.
  • Discount breakeven: the discount at which a 2- or 3-year deal yields the same expected revenue as annual renewals at typical churn — about 9% for 2 years and 13% for 3 years (The SaaS CFO). Deals above it are buying term with margin.

External benchmarks used

  • Multi-year share of SaaS contracts rose from ~25% (2022) to ~30% (2025) across 15,000+ contracts; buyers typically get only 2–3 pts more discount for multi-year (Mostly Metrics / Tropic).
  • Breakeven discounts of ~9% (2-yr) and ~13% (3-yr) vs annual, assuming equal renewal rates (The SaaS CFO).
  • Longer contracts defer churn more than they prevent it; median NRR ~102% and GRR ~91% across 1,500+ SaaS companies (SaaStr on SaaS Capital). That's why this page tracks churn by prior term, not just MY rate.
  • Annual price escalators of 3–10% are common, with 5% often the standard and 3–5% where customers push back (Blossom Street Ventures).
