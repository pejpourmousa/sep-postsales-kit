Methodology & benchmarks

Definitions

  • Upsell: more of a product line the account already owns (seats / licenses, usage / volume, tier upgrade). Cross-sell: a product line the account does not own yet. If your CSV has no motion column, the page classifies each deal by checking product_line against existing_products.
  • Forecast categories: Closed (won), Commit, Best Case, Pipeline; Omitted and closed-lost deals are excluded. Commit forecast = Closed + Commit. Best case = Closed + Commit + Best Case.
  • Weighted forecast: Closed + Σ(open ARR × probability). Missing probabilities default to Commit 80%, Best Case 50%, Pipeline 20%.
  • Trend forecast: Closed + Σ(open ARR × trailing-12-month win rate for that motion). A cross-check that ignores rep judgement.
  • Target: the annual target prorated by the number of months in the period. It's hidden while product, rep, segment, region or motion filters are on, because it is a company-level number.
  • Pipeline coverage: open (unweighted) pipeline closing in the period ÷ the gap still to close (target − closed won). A 3× rule of thumb is common for new logos; expansion usually needs less because win rates are higher.
  • Win rate (T12M): won ÷ (won + lost) for deals closed in the 12 months to the as-of date, by count or by ARR per the Measure toggle.
  • Cycle time: close date − created date for closed-won deals. Expansion rate: trailing-12-month closed-won expansion ARR ÷ Book ARR (entered above).

External benchmarks used

  • Expansion ARR was about 40% of total new ARR in 2024, rising to over 50% for companies above $50M ARR (58% median at $50–100M, 67% above $100M). Expansion CAC ratio median was $1.00 vs $2.00 for new customers (Benchmarkit 2025 SaaS Performance Metrics).
  • Median NRR around 101% and GRR around 88%, so expansion is what separates top performers (Benchmarkit). Private B2B SaaS with $25–50K ACVs show median NRR of about 102% (SaaS Capital).

Data

Sample data is synthetic and generated in the browser. Load a CSV of expansion opportunities (one row per upsell or cross-sell opportunity, open and closed) to use your own book. Required columns: opportunity_id, account_id, stage, close_date, arr, product_line, rep, plus either motion or existing_products. Optional: account_name, forecast_category, probability, created_date, existing_products (semicolon-separated, anchor product first), upsell_lever, segment, region, account_arr. arr is the incremental ARR of the expansion, not the account total. Everything runs locally in the browser, so no data leaves the page.
